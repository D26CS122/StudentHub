About StudentHub

One portal for your entire campus life

StudentHub brings attendance, results, events, profiles and campus announcements together behind a single login — designed by CHARUSAT students, for CHARUSAT students. Fast, focused and built to make everyday academic life effortless.

Why we built it

One portal, because four was three too many

Attendance in one place, results in another, event sign-ups on a notice board and everything else in a group chat. StudentHub replaced all of it with a single, modern portal.

Our mission

Cut the time a student spends chasing information down to nothing. Every record, form, notice and event lives behind one login — clear, current and always a click away, on any device.

Our vision

A campus where nobody misses a deadline for reading the wrong board. Where academics, events and student services feel as simple and polished as the apps students already love to use.

What StudentHub does

Everything, in one clean place

One login, every service

Attendance, CGPA, assignments, fees, events and announcements — all published to a single dashboard that's always up to date.

Attendance that warns you early

Subject-wise percentages refresh the same day and flag you before shortfall becomes a problem — not after the list goes up.

Records that stay with you

Marksheets, receipts, certificates and event history remain downloadable long after the semester ends.

Built the way you like it

Switch between the Campus Teal and Midnight Blue themes anytime — your choice is remembered on every page you open.

Our journey

How StudentHub came together

  1. 2024 · Q1

    The idea takes shape

    Frustrated by scattered tools, a group of IT students sketched a single portal to hold attendance, results, events and more.

  2. 2024 · Q3

    StudentHub goes live

    The first release brought the dashboard, profile and event registration online — retiring the old paper and spreadsheet flow.

  3. 2025 · Q2

    A premium redesign

    Glassmorphism, smooth animations and the dual Campus Teal / Midnight Blue themes gave the portal its signature look.

  4. 2026 · Today

    Trusted campus-wide

    Now used by 1200+ students every day for academics, events and everything in between — and still improving with your feedback.
