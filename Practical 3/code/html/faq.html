Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions about StudentHub.

How do I log in to StudentHub?

Click on the Student Login button on the home page and enter your Student ID and password.

How can I reset my password?

Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email.

Where can I check my attendance?

After logging in, open the Attendance page from the dashboard or sidebar menu.

How do I submit assignments?

Visit the Assignments page, choose the assignment, upload your file, and click Submit.

Where can I download study materials?

All lecture notes, PDFs, presentations, and other learning resources are available in the Study Material section.

How can I update my profile?

Open the Profile page from your dashboard, edit your information, and click Save Changes.

How do I contact the administrator?

Visit the Contact page and submit the contact form, or use the provided email and phone number.

Can I access StudentHub on my mobile phone?

Yes. StudentHub is fully responsive and works on desktops, tablets, and smartphones.
